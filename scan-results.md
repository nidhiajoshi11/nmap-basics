# Scan Results

## Basic Scan

Performed a scan on localhost to identify open ports and active services.

## Service Detection

Used service version detection to gather additional information about running services.

## Key Findings

* Identified open TCP ports.
* Observed active services running on the system.
* Learned how Nmap is used for network reconnaissance and enumeration.

## Conclusion

This project provided hands-on experience with Nmap and introduced the fundamentals of port scanning and service discovery.
